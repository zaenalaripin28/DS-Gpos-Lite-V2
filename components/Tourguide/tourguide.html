Tourguide — Component

Komponen tourguide menggabungkan overlay, target highlight, body konten, progress, dan action dari part yang sudah ada.

Part Class / Source
Overlay blanded .tourguide-component-stage__overlay → color/neutral/N800 50%
Target highlight .tourguide-component-stage__target
Action secondary .ds-btn-spotlight
Action primary replay .ds-btn-onboarding-replay

Title

Quickly switch between your most recent projects by selecting the project name and icon.

1/3

HTML siap copy — Tourguide Component

<div class="tourguide-component-stage">
  <div class="tourguide-component-stage__overlay"></div>
  <div class="tourguide-component-stage__target" aria-hidden="true"></div>
  <article class="ds-tourguide" aria-label="Tourguide">
    <div class="ds-tourguide__body">
      <h3 class="ds-tourguide__title">Title</h3>
      <p class="ds-tourguide__text">Quickly switch between your most recent projects by selecting the project name and icon.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ds-tourguide__footer">
      <span class="ds-tourguide__progress">1/3</span>
      <button type="button" class="ds-btn-spotlight">Dismiss</button>
      <button type="button" class="ds-btn-onboarding-replay">
        <img src="../../assets/icons/icon-arrow-uturn-left.svg" alt="arrowuturnleft" width="24" height="24">
        <span>Replay</span>
      </button>
    </div>
  </article>
</div>

Do

  • Gunakan judul singkat dan instruksi satu kalimat agar cepat dipahami.
  • Pastikan target highlight berada tepat di elemen yang dijelaskan.
  • Gunakan progress (contoh: 1/3) untuk memberi konteks langkah.
  • Pertahankan kontras overlay agar fokus tetap di area tourguide.

Don't

  • Jangan menaruh lebih dari dua tombol aksi dalam satu step.
  • Jangan gunakan teks instruksi yang panjang atau multi-paragraf.
  • Jangan ubah warna di luar token dan warna existing komponen.
  • Jangan menutup elemen target dengan panel tourguide.
